Community Guidelines

Simple expectations that help owners, dogs and the wider community use shared spaces safely and respectfully.

Shared park expectations

  • Respect dogs marked Needs Space, On Lead, In Training or Reactive.
  • Do not leave dogs unattended.
  • Do not harass, shame or threaten other owners.
  • Report hazards calmly and accurately.
  • Follow council signs and emergency services.
