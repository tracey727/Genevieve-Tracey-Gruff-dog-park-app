Animal interactions are unpredictable

No behavioural profile, compatibility percentage, colour or alert can guarantee how an animal will behave. Outputs depend on the information available at the time and may be incomplete, delayed, incorrect or affected by changing conditions.

Required human control

Owners and handlers must directly observe their animals, remain present, maintain effective control, follow council rules and use their own judgement. A green result means lower predicted risk based on available inputs—not “safe” or “approved”.

Veterinary and emergency boundaries

The service does not diagnose aggression, illness, pain, anxiety or other medical or behavioural conditions. Seek a veterinarian or qualified behaviour professional where appropriate. In a life-threatening emergency in Australia, call 000 and follow official directions.

Demonstration data

Park records included in the deployment are demonstration records and must not be advertised as current verified council data until independently verified and maintained.
