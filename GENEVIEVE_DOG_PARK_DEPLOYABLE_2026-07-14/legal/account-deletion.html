Local demonstration data

Open the app, select Settings, export any information you wish to retain, then choose “Delete all local app data”. This removes the app data stored in that browser on that device.

Production account deletion

When production accounts are enabled, users will be able to request deletion from Settings or through the published support address. Identity verification may be required to prevent unauthorised deletion.

What is deleted

Account details, owner-controlled dog profiles, preferences, social-affinity relationships and ordinary app records will be deleted or de-identified, except where information must be retained for security, fraud prevention, transaction, dispute, legal or regulatory purposes.

Subscriptions

Account deletion and app deletion do not cancel an Apple, Google Play or Stripe subscription. Cancel through the original billing channel first.
