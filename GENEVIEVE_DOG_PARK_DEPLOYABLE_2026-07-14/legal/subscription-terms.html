After the trial, the selected price is charged every month or year and automatically renews until cancelled. The exact local price shown by Apple or Google Play controls for store purchases.

Concession products must not be publicly activated until an eligibility standard, minimal evidence process, privacy notice, review process and non-discrimination safeguards are finalised.

Premium access continues only while the relevant subscription remains active. Store purchases should support restoration. Cross-platform access requires secure server-side verification before it can be promised.

Price or product changes will be handled according to the billing provider’s rules and applicable law, with required notice or consent.
