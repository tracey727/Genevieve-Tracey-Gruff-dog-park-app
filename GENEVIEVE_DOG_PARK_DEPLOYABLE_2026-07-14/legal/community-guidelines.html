Respect and safety

  • Do not harass, threaten, shame or identify another person without lawful authority.
  • Do not publish private contact, precise-location, medical, microchip or emergency information.
  • Do not knowingly submit false incident, behaviour or location information.
  • Do not use compatibility output to pressure another owner into an interaction.
  • Respect requests for space, incognito settings and relationship removal.
  • Use official council, veterinary and emergency channels where required.

Moderation

Content or accounts may be restricted where reasonably necessary to address safety, privacy, unlawful conduct, fraud or repeated misuse. A fair review process should be added before public community posting is enabled.
