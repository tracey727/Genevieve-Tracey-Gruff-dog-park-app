1. Scope

This policy explains how the GENEVIEVE App™ Dog Park website and future mobile applications handle information relating to app users, dog profiles, park visits, social-affinity relationships, reports and subscriptions.

2. Information that may be collected

  • Account and contact information supplied by the user.
  • Owner-controlled dog profile information, behavioural observations, photographs and notes.
  • Check-in, approximate or precise location information where the user separately enables it.
  • Affinity relationships, notification preferences and private removal feedback.
  • Compatibility predictions, observed outcomes and audit records.
  • Subscription status and transaction references supplied by Apple, Google Play or Stripe. Full card details are not intended to be stored by GENEVIEVE App™.
  • Technical information such as browser, device, diagnostic and security logs when production services are enabled.

3. Purposes

Information may be used to provide the requested app functions, secure accounts, synchronise data, deliver owner-configured notifications, calculate decision-support outputs, administer subscriptions, investigate misuse, respond to support requests, improve the service using permitted feedback and comply with law.

4. Location and visibility controls

Precise location is optional unless a clearly identified future feature requires it. Users can separately control discoverability, live-presence visibility, affinity alerts, recommendations and learning participation. Incognito check-ins are excluded from affinity-alert results.

5. Service providers and overseas processing

Production hosting, authentication, analytics, email, mapping and payment providers have not all been finalised. The final policy must identify the providers, storage regions and likely overseas disclosures before collection begins.

6. Security and retention

Production services must use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, access controls, encryption in transit, credential management, logging and retention rules. Information no longer required for a lawful purpose should be deleted or de-identified, subject to lawful retention requirements.

7. Access, correction and deletion

Users may export or delete local demonstration data from Settings. Production account access, correction and deletion requests will be handled through the published support channel and the account-deletion page.

8. Complaints and contact

A public support email and complaint process must be published before launch. Privacy complaints will be assessed and responded to within a reasonable period.
