GENEVIEVE Dog Park

SUBTITLE
Safer dog park planning

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Plan visits, compare dog profiles and park conditions, manage best-mate alerts and use clearly labelled safety prompts.

KEYWORDS
dog park,dog profile,pet safety,off leash,dog compatibility,dog travel,pet alerts

DESCRIPTION
GENEVIEVE App™ Dog Park helps dog owners prepare for dog park visits with owner-controlled dog profiles, park information, compatibility decision support, check-in tools and privacy controls.

Select two dog profiles and a park to review a graduated compatibility result with written reasons and environmental adjustments. Record outcomes, manage best-mate relationships and choose whether presence or alerts are visible.

Features may include:
• owner-controlled dog profiles
• behavioural compatibility guidance
• prospective companion recommendations
• preferred-park best-mate alerts
• check-in and check-out
• labelled green, yellow, amber and red guidance
• privacy, export and account-deletion controls
• park-planning and owner-supervision reminders

Important: GENEVIEVE App™ supports safer decisions but does not guarantee behaviour, compatibility, availability, legal compliance or data accuracy. Owners remain responsible for their dogs and must follow official rules, signs and emergency directions.

Some features require an auto-renewing subscription. The subscription period, local price, trial terms, included access, automatic renewal and cancellation method are displayed before purchase.
