Staged introduction checklist
  1. Observe both dogs separately before approaching.
  2. Start with distance and loose leads outside the gate where lawful and safe.
  3. Watch for stiffness, avoidance, fixed staring, escalating arousal or inability to disengage.
  4. Use a brief parallel walk or calm sniff break before closer contact.
  5. Stop immediately if either dog, owner or handler is uncomfortable.
  6. Reassess after any environmental change, crowd surge, heat increase or resource conflict.

A green result never means guaranteed compatibility.

Immediate safety actions before making a report
  1. Create distance and secure dogs without placing yourself at further risk.
  2. Check people and animals for urgent injury.
  3. For immediate danger or serious injury, use the Triple Zero hold-and-slide control.
  4. Preserve photos, times, locations and witness details only when safe and lawful.
