Ownership and IP boundary

Founder and owner: Tracey Ann Kennedy. Pre-existing GENEVIEVE App™ software, designs, written materials, branding and associated patent rights remain with their lawful owner. No app use or pilot record transfers ownership.

Important safety boundary

GENEVIEVE App™ supports decisions only. It does not guarantee safety, replace handler judgment, classify legal dangerousness, replace veterinary or professional advice, dispatch help or verify third-party information.

Maintenance responsibility

Councils and facility operators retain responsibility for infrastructure, fencing, gates, signs, inspection, repair and site maintenance. Dog owners retain direct supervision and control. The app records observations; it does not perform or certify maintenance.
