Plans and advertised prices

  • Standard Monthly — A$14.99 each month
  • Concession Monthly — A$10.49 each month
  • Standard Annual — A$119.99 each year

A product is not available until its approved provider product and exact purchase sheet are connected and verified.

Trial and automatic renewal

An eligible new subscriber may receive a 30-day trial only when the provider purchase screen confirms eligibility and states the price, currency, renewal interval and cancellation method. Unless cancelled through the original provider before renewal, the displayed recurring price may be charged.

Cancellation, restore and entitlement

Apple subscriptions are managed in Apple subscription settings; Google subscriptions in Google Play; and a future Stripe web subscription through a secure hosted customer portal. Deleting the app or local data does not cancel. Native builds must restore purchases through supported store billing. Access must be based on verified entitlement, not a local button state.

Consumer rights

Nothing excludes rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Contact tracey@genevieveapp.com.au for billing support.
