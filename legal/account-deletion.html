Account & Data Deletion

Version: 3 August 2026 · Current build: no server account

Delete current local data

Open More → Your data → Delete all local app data. Confirming removes the GENEVIEVE Dog Parks browser record on that device and restores the starter profile. The app cannot erase copies the user exported, browser backups, or information sent to third-party maps, weather, app-store or payment providers.

Future accounts

If account creation or cloud synchronisation is enabled, the app must provide an in-app path to initiate account deletion and a public web request route. Associated data must be deleted unless retention is required by law, security, fraud prevention, financial-record or dispute obligations. Any retained category and period must be explained.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting data or an account does not cancel Apple, Google Play or Stripe billing. Cancel through the original provider first.

Account launch blocker: do not enable account creation until authenticated deletion, identity verification, status confirmation, provider-token revocation where relevant, public deletion URL, support contact and retention rules are implemented and tested.
