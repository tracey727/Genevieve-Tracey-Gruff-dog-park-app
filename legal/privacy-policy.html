Privacy Policy

Version: 3 August 2026 · Operator: Tracey Ann Kennedy trading as GENEVIEVE App™ · Privacy contact: tracey@genevieveapp.com.au

Information used by this build

  • Dog profile, behaviour, health, veterinarian, microchip and emergency-contact details entered by the user.
  • Voluntary check-ins, assessments, plans, preferences and legal acceptance stored in that browser.
  • Device location only after the user requests it and grants browser permission.
  • Selected park coordinates sent to the weather provider only when weather is requested.
  • A place or current coordinates included in Google Maps search links only when the user opens nearby parks, vets, pounds, council animal services, rescues, wildlife help or other external services.

The current PWA is local-first and does not create a server account. Emergency-search coordinates are kept only in page memory, are not added to GENEVIEVE browser records and are cleared when the page closes or reloads. Google Maps receives the search only when the user opens a service link. No payment-card number is collected by this code.

Use, storage and disclosure

Information supports profiles, check-ins, safety guidance, accessibility settings, exports and deletion. Current records remain in browser storage unless the user exports them or opens a third-party feature. Google Maps, Open-Meteo, the browser, hosting and app-store providers may process technical or request data under their own policies. Cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, messaging or community sync must not be enabled until this policy names the final provider and processing.

Access, correction, deletion and complaints

Users can edit profiles, export local records or delete them under More → Data & Account. Email tracey@genevieveapp.com.au for a privacy complaint. Future server accounts require authenticated access, correction and deletion processes plus published retention periods.

Security, children and changes

Users should protect their device and avoid entering sensitive details on a shared device. The service is for dog owners and supervisors; children should use it only with a parent or guardian. Material changes receive a new version date and any consent required by law.

Before paid/account launch: add the verified service address and ABN/ACN if applicable, final provider list, overseas-processing facts, complaint timeframe, retention schedule, breach process and Australian legal review.
