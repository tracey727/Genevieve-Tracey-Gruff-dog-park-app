Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version: 3 August 2026 · Billing support: tracey@genevieveapp.com.au

Cancellation

Cancel through the provider used to subscribe: Apple subscription settings, Google Play subscription settings, or the secure Stripe customer portal if web billing is enabled. Cancellation generally stops future renewal; deleting the app or local data does not cancel.

Trials and refunds

Cancel before the deadline displayed by the provider to avoid the first charge. Apple- and Google-processed purchases use their refund processes. A Stripe purchase uses the published support route. Provider processes do not remove Australian Consumer Law rights.

Support

Keep the receipt, product, date, account identifier and description of the problem, then email tracey@genevieveapp.com.au.

Before paid launch: publish provider-specific cancellation/refund links, a response timeframe, refund authority process, service address and Australian legal review.
