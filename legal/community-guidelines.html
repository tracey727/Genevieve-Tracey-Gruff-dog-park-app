Community Guidelines

Version: 3 August 2026 · Current build: local-first; community sync disabled

Share only accurate, necessary park-safety information. Do not harass, shame, threaten, impersonate, diagnose or identify another person or dog; publish private contact, location, health or account information; organise unsafe encounters; or submit unlawful, discriminatory, misleading or commercial spam.

Check-ins are voluntary signals, not reservations, guarantees, official ranger information or permission to approach. Users remain responsible for direct observation, signs, council rules and effective control.

Report a product or safety concern to tracey@genevieveapp.com.au. In an immediate emergency call 000.

Before community sync: implement reporting, blocking, moderation, appeals, evidence retention, abuse response, child safety, privacy controls and tested administrator access.
