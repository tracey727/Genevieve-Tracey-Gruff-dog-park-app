Privacy Policy

How GENEVIEVE App™ Dog Park handles owner, dog, location, safety and emergency information.

i

Draft for legal review.

This page is included for deployment review. GENEVIEVE App™ is designed around consent, minimal collection, optional safety sharing and private dog records.

Information used by this app

  • Owner profile and chosen safety contacts.
  • Dog profile, temperament, triggers, medical notes and emergency profile.
  • Check-in and check-out actions and optional location context.
  • Incident, lost and found, and safety reports.

Private by default

Microchip details, medical notes and emergency contacts are restricted by default and should only be shared by the owner or where legally required in an emergency.
